About Afraah Tech Hub

Empowering people with digital skills for a sustainable future.

Who We Are

Afraah Tech Hub is a forward-thinking innovation hub based in Funtua, Nigeria. We are committed to building a digital economy by equipping individuals and organizations with cutting-edge technology skills, practical knowledge, and real-world exposure.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of excellence where ideas are transformed into innovations, and talents are nurtured into global tech leaders.

Our Mission

To empower young people, businesses, and communities through capacity-building, innovation, and access to technology for sustainable development.

Our Core Values

  • Innovation – Pioneering new ideas and solutions.
  • Excellence – Striving for quality in everything we do.
  • Integrity – Operating with transparency and accountability.
  • Community – Building networks that uplift society.
  • Sustainability – Focusing on long-term impact and growth.

Leadership

Afraah Tech Hub is led by passionate innovators, educators, and entrepreneurs with expertise spanning law, technology, design, and community development. Our team works tirelessly to bridge the gap between digital skills and market needs.
